Radio focus test

Two groups side by side (like a WheelGroup), radios stacked vertically (like the y-axis focus group). Each group has a checked value that is not the first, and one disabled value. Tab from the anchor into the groups, use ↑/↓ inside, and Tab across to the next group. Compare the two sections and watch:
  • Tab entry: checked value, or first/last?
  • Tab across: what happens between the two groups?
  • Arrow nav: does it skip the disabled value?
