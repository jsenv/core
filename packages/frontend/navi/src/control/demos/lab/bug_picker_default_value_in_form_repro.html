Picker on a defaultValue, inside a Form

A tie-break cell: a type="object" picker named set1_tie_break, sitting on the most likely score (7-4), in a form the score sheet drives with a signal. The cell is mounted by Mount the cell, the way the real sheet mounts it when a set stands one game apart.
Open the popup and press Noter without turning a wheel — the form value below must gain set1_tie_break: {"{"} winner: 7, loser: 4 {"}"}, and the form's uiAction must fire exactly once. This holds whichever order the two gestures come in: mounting the cell before or after typing in set1_winner. Escape cancels and must leave the form alone, and so must never opening the popup at all: a suggestion is not an answer until it is confirmed.
