Tap-after-drag suppression

No navi, no framework — just a bare touch-action: none, overflow: hidden surface with pointer listeners that translate an inner block.
To reproduce: drag the grey surface up/down, then within ~500ms tap the blue Tap me button. Watch for TARGET click. If it is missing, the browser suppressed the click after the drag — proving the behavior is native, not navi.
